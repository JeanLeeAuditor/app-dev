 Me Before You

My favorite movie is Me Before You, a romantic drama film released in 2016. It stars Emilia Clarke as Louisa Clark and Sam Claflin as Will Traynor.

The story is about Louisa, a cheerful young woman who becomes a caregiver for Will, a wealthy man who became paralyzed after an accident. As they spend time together, they develop a special connection and slowly fall in love.

I like this movie because it shows the meaning of love, kindness, sacrifice, and appreciating life. It is also emotional and teaches us that even a short time with someone can make a big difference in our lives.

For me, Me Before You is a memorable movie because it reminds me to value every moment, follow my dreams, and appreciate the people who make life meaningful.
